Guidance for S / O / D Scoring (FMEVA)
  • Severity (S): Impact if failure occurs (10 = grid instability, safety risk, national disruption)
  • Occurrence (O): Likelihood given architecture & exposure (10 = frequent / highly exposed)
  • Detection (D): Ability to detect before impact (10 = no visibility, late detection)
Rule of thumb: Detection dominates cyber-physical cascades.
